# City of Legends - Visual Concept Guide

## 🎨 Map Visualization & Design Concepts

This document describes the visual design and aesthetic of the City of Legends roleplay map.

---

## 🏙️ Overall Aesthetic

### Design Philosophy
**"Modern Urban Meets Fortnite Style"**

City of Legends combines realistic city planning with Fortnite's vibrant, accessible art style. The map should feel like a living, breathing city while maintaining the playful energy that Fortnite is known for.

### Color Palette

#### Downtown Financial District
- **Primary Colors**: Steel gray, glass blue, professional white
- **Accent Colors**: Gold trim, corporate blue, neon signs
- **Atmosphere**: Clean, modern, prestigious
- **Lighting**: Bright during day, neon glow at night
- **Materials**: Glass, polished stone, steel, concrete

#### Industrial Zone
- **Primary Colors**: Rust brown, weathered metal gray, dark concrete
- **Accent Colors**: Yellow warning stripes, safety orange
- **Atmosphere**: Gritty, functional, working-class
- **Lighting**: Harsh industrial lights, shadows
- **Materials**: Corrugated metal, exposed brick, chain-link fencing

#### Residential Neighborhoods
- **Primary Colors**: Warm beige, suburban green, family blue
- **Accent Colors**: Colorful gardens, varied house colors
- **Atmosphere**: Cozy, welcoming, lived-in
- **Lighting**: Soft street lamps, home lighting
- **Materials**: Wood, brick, vinyl siding, grass

#### Entertainment District
- **Primary Colors**: Vibrant purple, neon pink, electric blue
- **Accent Colors**: Gold accents, laser effects, RGB lighting
- **Atmosphere**: Energetic, exciting, social
- **Lighting**: Dynamic neon, spotlights, club lighting
- **Materials**: Modern glass, LED panels, polished surfaces

#### Emergency Services Hub
- **Primary Colors**: Authority blue (police), medical red (hospital), fire red/yellow
- **Accent Colors**: Warning yellow, official white
- **Atmosphere**: Organized, professional, secure
- **Lighting**: Bright institutional lighting, emergency lights
- **Materials**: Reinforced concrete, security glass, steel

#### Outskirts & Countryside
- **Primary Colors**: Natural green, earth brown, sky blue
- **Accent Colors**: Gas station colors, road signs
- **Atmosphere**: Open, peaceful, transitional
- **Lighting**: Natural sunlight, highway lights
- **Materials**: Asphalt, grass, trees, rural structures

## 🏢 Key Landmark Designs

### 1. Police Station - "CLPD Headquarters"

**Exterior Design**:
- 3-story modern police building
- Blue and white color scheme with reflective glass
- Large "CITY OF LEGENDS POLICE DEPARTMENT" sign on front
- Vehicle bay with 4 garage doors
- Helipad on roof (if available in Fortnite)
- Security cameras and lighting
- American flag and city flag poles

**Interior Design**:
- **Ground Floor**:
  - Front desk with reception
  - Lobby with seating
  - Evidence room with storage
  - Interview rooms (2)
  - Armory with weapon racks
- **Second Floor**:
  - Offices for officers and detectives
  - Captain's office (corner office with windows)
  - Briefing room with chairs and whiteboard
  - Break room
- **Basement**:
  - Jail cells (3 cells with metal bars/barriers)
  - Booking area with mugshot backdrop
  - Processing desk

**Atmosphere**:
- Professional, secure, authoritative
- Blue accent lighting
- Police equipment visible (badges, radios, computers)
- Wanted posters on walls
- City map on wall showing districts

### 2. Hospital - "City of Legends Medical Center"

**Exterior Design**:
- Modern 4-story medical building
- White and red cross color scheme
- Large "EMERGENCY" sign at entrance
- Ambulance bay with covered entrance
- Parking lot with designated EMS spaces
- Red cross logo prominently displayed

**Interior Design**:
- **Ground Floor**:
  - Emergency entrance and triage
  - Reception desk
  - Waiting room with chairs
  - Treatment rooms (4-6 rooms with beds)
  - Medical supply storage
- **Upper Floors** (if space allows):
  - Operating room
  - Recovery rooms
  - Staff offices
  - Cafeteria

**Atmosphere**:
- Clean, sterile, professional
- White walls with red accents
- Medical equipment visible (stretchers, IV stands, monitors)
- Health safety posters
- Hand sanitizer stations

### 3. Fire Station - "Station 1"

**Exterior Design**:
- 2-story traditional fire station
- Red and white color scheme
- Large garage doors (3 bays)
- "FIRE DEPARTMENT" lettering
- Bell or siren on roof
- Emergency vehicle parking

**Interior Design**:
- **Ground Floor**:
  - Fire truck bays (open garage)
  - Gear storage (helmets, axes, suits on racks)
  - Equipment room
  - Dispatch area
- **Second Floor**:
  - Living quarters
  - Kitchen and dining
  - Fire pole (if possible in Fortnite)
  - Recreation area
  - Chief's office

**Atmosphere**:
- Functional, ready-for-action
- Red and brass accents
- Fire equipment displayed
- Motivational firefighter posters
- Emergency response maps

### 4. City Hall - "Municipal Building & Employment Center"

**Exterior Design**:
- Grand 3-story government building
- Classical architecture with columns
- American flag and city flag at entrance
- Wide stairs leading to entrance
- "CITY HALL" engraved above doors

**Interior Design**:
- **Ground Floor**:
  - Grand lobby with high ceiling
  - **Employment Center**: 10 job selection stations with signs
  - Information desk
  - Seating areas
- **Second Floor**:
  - Mayor's office (luxury corner office)
  - City council chambers
  - Government offices
  - Courtroom (for trials)
- **Third Floor**:
  - Records department
  - Meeting rooms

**Atmosphere**:
- Official, prestigious, welcoming
- Marble or polished floors
- Gold and blue accents
- Government seals and symbols
- Job posters on walls: "JOIN THE CLPD!", "WE'RE HIRING: EMS TEAM", etc.

### 5. Bank - "Legend City Bank"

**Exterior Design**:
- Imposing 2-story financial building
- Stone and glass construction
- "LEGEND CITY BANK" in gold letters
- Revolving doors at entrance
- ATM machines outside
- Security cameras visible

**Interior Design**:
- **Ground Floor**:
  - Customer service area with teller windows
  - 4 ATM stations
  - Seating area for waiting customers
  - Security guard station
- **Vault Area**:
  - Massive vault door (for heist scenarios)
  - Safe deposit boxes
  - Security office

**Atmosphere**:
- Professional, secure, wealthy
- Gold and navy blue color scheme
- Marble floors and columns
- Money-themed decorations
- Security presence

### 6. Car Dealership - "Legend Motors"

**Exterior Design**:
- Modern glass showroom
- Large "LEGEND MOTORS" sign with car logo
- Outdoor lot with vehicles on display
- Colorful flags and banners
- Service bay attached

**Interior Design**:
- Showroom with 5-8 vehicles displayed
- Sales office with desk
- Finance office
- Waiting area with comfortable seating
- Coffee station
- Vehicle customization area

**Atmosphere**:
- Sleek, modern, inviting
- Bright showroom lighting
- Car posters and promotional materials
- Price tags on vehicles
- "SALE!" and "NEW ARRIVAL!" signs

## 🚗 Vehicle Designs (Using Fortnite Vehicles)

### Personal Vehicles
1. **Economy Car** (Basic Sedan)
   - Fortnite's standard car
   - Neutral colors (white, gray, beige)
   - Affordable starter vehicle

2. **Sports Car** (High-Performance)
   - Fortnite's sports car or modified vehicle
   - Vibrant colors (red, yellow, blue)
   - Sleek design with speed boost appearance

3. **SUV/Truck** (Utility Vehicle)
   - Fortnite's truck or large vehicle
   - Rugged appearance
   - Earth tones (brown, green, black)

### Job Vehicles
1. **Police Patrol Car**
   - Modified Fortnite car with blue/white livery
   - Emergency lights on top (if possible)
   - "POLICE" decals on doors
   - Police badge on hood

2. **Ambulance**
   - White vehicle with red cross
   - "EMS" or "AMBULANCE" lettering
   - Red emergency lights
   - Medical symbol

3. **Fire Truck**
   - Red Fortnite vehicle (largest available)
   - "FIRE DEPT" on sides
   - Ladder or equipment visible
   - Emergency lights and sirens

4. **Taxi**
   - Yellow Fortnite car
   - "TAXI" sign on top
   - Checkered pattern
   - Meter inside (cosmetic)

## 🌆 Atmosphere & Ambiance

### Time of Day Settings
1. **Daytime** (Default):
   - Bright sunlight
   - Active city with NPCs (if possible)
   - Business hours vibe
   - Clear visibility

2. **Nighttime** (Optional cycle):
   - Neon lights activated
   - Street lamps on
   - Building lights glow
   - Entertainment district comes alive
   - More mysterious atmosphere

### Environmental Details
- **Traffic Lights**: At major intersections (red/yellow/green)
- **Street Signs**: Directional signs, district markers
- **Billboards**: Advertisements for in-game businesses
- **Benches**: Public seating throughout
- **Trash Cans**: City realism
- **Plants**: Trees, bushes, flowers in residential areas
- **Sidewalks**: Defined pedestrian areas

## 🎭 Character Appearance Guide

### Job-Specific Visual Identity

#### Police Officer
- **Uniform**: Dark blue outfit
- **Equipment**: Badge, belt with tools, radio, hat
- **Vehicles**: Police car, motorcycle
- **Presence**: Authoritative, professional

#### Paramedic
- **Uniform**: White or light blue medical scrubs
- **Equipment**: Medical bag, stethoscope
- **Vehicles**: Ambulance
- **Presence**: Caring, professional

#### Firefighter
- **Uniform**: Fire-resistant gear, helmet
- **Equipment**: Axe, oxygen tank
- **Vehicles**: Fire truck
- **Presence**: Brave, heroic

#### Business Professional
- **Attire**: Suit and tie, professional dress
- **Equipment**: Briefcase, phone
- **Vehicles**: Luxury sedan
- **Presence**: Sophisticated, successful

#### Taxi Driver
- **Attire**: Casual work clothes
- **Equipment**: Clipboard, radio
- **Vehicles**: Yellow taxi cab
- **Presence**: Friendly, hardworking

#### Criminal (Optional RP)
- **Attire**: Dark clothes, mask (during crimes)
- **Equipment**: Varies by activity
- **Vehicles**: Fast cars, modified vehicles
- **Presence**: Sneaky, dangerous

## 🏆 Visual Storytelling Elements

### Environmental Storytelling Props
1. **Police Station**: Wanted posters, crime board with photos/strings
2. **Hospital**: Health safety posters, patient charts
3. **City Hall**: Job opportunity boards, city maps
4. **Streets**: "HELP WANTED" signs, business advertisements
5. **Properties**: "FOR SALE" signs, "SOLD" markers

### Activity Indicators
- **Working**: Glowing outline or badge above player
- **Wanted**: Star icons or red glow
- **Emergency**: Flashing lights and sirens
- **Property Owner**: Key icon or nameplate

## 🎨 Building Style Guide

### Architecture Principles
1. **Realism**: Buildings should look functional and believable
2. **Scale**: Maintain consistent scale for immersion
3. **Detail**: Add small details that tell stories
4. **Navigation**: Clear pathways and signage
5. **Accessibility**: Easy to find and enter buildings

### Color Coding by Function
- **Government Buildings**: Blue and white
- **Medical Facilities**: White and red
- **Emergency Services**: Bright red and yellow
- **Commercial**: Various bright colors
- **Residential**: Warm, varied colors
- **Industrial**: Gray and brown tones
